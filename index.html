F1 Social Publisher

F1 Social Publisher is a web-based social media management workspace for authorized businesses, creators and client teams. It centralizes connected social accounts, content preparation, review and publishing workflows in one place.

What the service does

Authorized users can connect supported social accounts, prepare content, review publishing information and send approved content to the selected social platform. Connections use the authorization methods made available by each platform.

  • Multi-client social account organization.
  • Content preparation and publishing workflows.
  • Connection status and reauthorization management.
  • Support for platform-specific publishing permissions.

TikTok integration

When a user chooses to connect TikTok, F1 Social Publisher uses TikTok Login Kit for account authorization and the TikTok Content Posting API for supported publishing actions. Only TikTok accounts explicitly authorized by their owner or administrator are connected.
